MODEL DEFINITION

What the app calculates

Portfolio Kelly budget

Full, half, and quarter Kelly set the maximum total capital budget across all open trades. Half Kelly can never silently drift back toward a Full-Kelly exposure.

Target trade size

The portfolio budget is divided by the expected number of overlapping trades, estimated from opportunities per week × average holding days ÷ 7. Actual arrivals and holding periods still vary randomly.

Optional margin

Margin off caps total exposure at 100% of equity. Margin on permits up to 150%. If the Kelly formula is below those limits, the Kelly budget remains lower. Margin interest is not included.

Annual taxes & withdrawals

Optional tax withholding is charged every 365 simulated days on positive realized trading P&L from that year. A fixed optional withdrawal is then deducted. Losing years do not generate a tax credit.

Risk of ruin

Risk of ruin is the percentage of simulated paths that touch your chosen dollar threshold before reaching the target within the selected horizon.

Important: Trade opportunities arrive randomly around your weekly average, holding periods vary around the entered average, and open positions tie up capital until exit. The model still assumes trade outcomes are independent and each closed trade equals the entered average win or loss. If equity falls enough that existing positions exceed the selected Kelly budget, the model proportionally trims open positions back to budget. Real results include wider outcome dispersion, slippage, gaps, margin interest, tax-law details, regime changes, and correlation.
