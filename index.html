MODEL DEFINITION

What the app calculates

Kelly allocation

The calculator maximizes expected logarithmic account growth using your win probability and percentage return on winning and losing positions.

No leverage

If the formula suggests placing more than the account balance into a trade, the app uses 100% of equity. Capital committed to an open trade is unavailable for new trades until that position closes.

Dollar R

1R equals the suggested bet amount multiplied by the average losing-trade percentage. A $100,000 bet with a 5% average loss has a $5,000 R.

Risk of ruin

Risk of ruin is the percentage of simulated paths that touch your chosen dollar threshold before reaching the target within the selected horizon.

Important: Trade opportunities arrive randomly around your weekly average, holding periods vary around the entered average, and open positions tie up capital until exit. The model still assumes trade outcomes are independent and each closed trade equals the entered average win or loss. Real results include wider outcome dispersion, slippage, gaps, regime changes, and correlation.
