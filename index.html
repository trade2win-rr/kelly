Practical rule

Kelly is an upper-bound growth model. Your hard risk cap should usually control the final number, especially when estimates are based on a small sample or market conditions are changing.

FORMULA

What the calculator is doing

For a strategy with win probability p, loss probability q = 1 − p, and payoff ratio b = average win ÷ average loss:

Full Kelly = (b × p − q) ÷ b

The result is the theoretical fraction of account equity to risk on each independent trade when a normal losing trade loses one unit of risk.

INTERPRETATION

Win rate is not your edge

A 60% win rate does not automatically mean a 60% edge. A strategy can win often and still lose money if its losers are much larger than its winners. The simulator combines both win probability and payoff ratio.

Expectancy = p × b − q

SIMULATION

How account paths are generated

Each path repeatedly draws a win or loss using your entered win rate. A win increases equity by the applied risk fraction multiplied by the payoff ratio. A loss reduces equity by the applied risk fraction.

The process stops when the path reaches the target or hits the maximum number of trades.

LIMITATIONS

What Kelly does not know

Real trades are not perfectly independent. Slippage, gaps, changing volatility, strategy decay, correlated positions, taxes, fees, and estimation error can all make realized results worse than the model.

Use historical data across enough trades, test different regimes, and favor fractional Kelly plus a conservative hard cap.

KellyLab is an educational modeling tool, not financial advice. Estimates are hypothetical and depend entirely on the assumptions entered.
